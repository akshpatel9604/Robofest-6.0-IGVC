# Rover Component List
Team Cognitrek, Dharmsinh Desai University
Robofest Gujarat 6.0 — IGVC Senior

## 1. Compute
- Raspberry Pi 5 (8 GB)
- Official Raspberry Pi 5 active cooler
- microSD card (64 GB or 128 GB)

## 2. Vision and sensing
- Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3
- CSI camera cable
- Ultrasonic sensors (3–4 nos., HC-SR04 or JSN-SR04T)
- Neo-6M GPS module + antenna
- IMU (MPU-6050 or BNO055) [optional]

## 3. Drive and actuation
- 6 DC gear motors
- High-current motor drivers (BTS7960 / Cytron class)
- PCA9685 PWM driver
- MG996R servo motors
- Wheel encoders [optional]

## 4. Power
- 12V 30Ah lithium battery
- 12V to 5V buck converter (5–8A)
- Wiring, XT60, fuse
- Emergency stop switch

## 5. Mechanical
- Aluminium extrusions 20 mm x 20 mm
- L-brackets and M4 nuts/bolts
- Pillow block bearings and flange bearings (10 mm)
- Steel carbon rods (10 mm)
- 6 balloon-type stripped rubber wheels (10–12 inch / 25–30 cm)
- Shock absorbers
- Aluminium sheets 3 mm
- Acrylic sheet for cover
- 3D-printed mounts (ABS / PLA)

## 6. Chassis size
- Length: 100 cm
- Width: 90 cm
- Height: 63 cm
- Track width: 680 mm
- Wheel diameter: 280 mm
- Ground clearance: 120 mm
